### Runner up
*2 link revolute arm is runner up.*
It is very convenient at all aspects but still it has more complex joint coordination.
Also due to its lower stiffness and greater positioning errors, it is not precise.

### Calculations

Selected mechanism: Linear lift with a short arm
*Proposed Dimensions*
- Initial gripper-centre height = 150 mm
- Vertical lift travel = 300 mm
- Short arm length = 80 mm
- Ground clearance = 50 mm

*Reach Verification*
The gripper needs to reach an object whose centre is 450 mm above the ground.
For my linear lift mechanism:
Maximum gripper height = Initial gripper height + Vertical lift travel
= 150 + 300
= 450 mm
The 80 mm short arm provides horizontal reach, so it does not affect the vertical height.
Since the calculated height is equal to the required 450 mm, the proposed mechanism meets the reach requirement.
