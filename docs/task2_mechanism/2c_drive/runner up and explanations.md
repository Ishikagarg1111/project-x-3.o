### Runner up
*2 finger linkage gripper is runner up*
because it offers less shape adaptability and grip reliability than the underactuated adaptive gripper.

### Actuator
I have chosen an MG90S servo motor to operate the gripper. A single servo will control both fingers through a linkage mechanism.
Working:
When the servo rotates, the fingers start closing around the object.
Since the fingers are underactuated, they can adjust their positions according to the object's shape instead of moving in one fixed way.

This makes the gripper suitable for holding cubes, cylinders and other irregularly shaped objects.

### Prevention from Slipping
Rubber pads on the inner surfaces of the fingers can be used to increase friction between the gripper and the object.
The adaptive fingers will also make contact at multiple points, helping distribute the gripping force and hold the object more securely.
This should reduce the chances of the object slipping while the robot moves or lifts it.
