# ProjectX-3.0
RoboReach — README

## Assumptions
- I assumed track surface to be smooth instead of a muddy or uneven terrain.
- The dimensions and component values are based on the initial design and may change after testing.
- Wheel diameter = 100 mm
- Wheelbase = 160 mm
- Track width = 160 mm
- Ground clearance = 50 mm
- Initial gripper-centre height = 150 mm
- Vertical lift travel = 300 mm
- Short arm length = 80 mm
- Battery = 3S LiPo (11.1 V nominal)

## Limitations

- The robot has not been physically built or tested so some decisions may be need to change later.
- A 4 wheel skid stir may cause problem that not all of its tyre touch the ground while crossing speed breaker
- Obstacle-crossing ability depends on motor torque, traction and weight distribution.
- The linear lift mechanism needs further verification to ensure it fits inside the starting box.
- Gripper performance with different objects has not been tested.Grip may not damage any object.
- Power calculations are estimates based on assumed errorless operating conditions.
